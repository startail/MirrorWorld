Dexterity cost in tiles:
- An additional way of balancing tiles
- Characters have a Dexterity stat
  - It could be upgraded with stuff bought from shops, items could temporarily boost dexterity
- Tiles have a Dexterity Cost stat
  - The number varies from tile to tile, ex a shuriken would have 1, meteor hammer has 3
  - The Dexterity Cost can be reduced with upgrades
  - When queueing tiles, if the total Dexterity Cost exceeds your Dexterity, the Tile that exceeded your Dexterity is performed after advancing one turn
  - If the total Dexterity Cost in your queue exceeds another multiple of your Dexterity (queue's total Dexterity Cost > Dexterity*2), the Tile that caused that to happen is performed after advancing another turn
