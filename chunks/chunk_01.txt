**Changing Difficulty modes into Gameplay Options. **

Currently, there are two modes: ***normal ***and ***hard***. Furthermore, there has been a lot of discussion about things like and Undo mechanic or similar, if they would be an option or tied to a difficulty mode or something else.

Instead, I would suggest ***Gameplay Options*** and an ascension based difficulty scale. Essentially, when you first play the game you start at Difficulty 5 (name could change). There are then a whole bunch of options (either available from the start, or after you first beat the game), which allow you to raise or lower the difficulty. These modifiers/bonuses could include:
> Starting with a non-wanderer character, Undo-per-Hour, Enemy health, certain enemies showing up earlier, things tied to enemy behavior, reroll cost, %chance for loot drops, etc.

There are a number of games that already use this to great effect, such as Revita's shard tier (<https://revitagame.fandom.com/wiki/Shards>). It makes it so that rather than difficulty going up a in a preset way, options can be used by the player to make a run harder or easier - and further it doesn't make it so players to enjoy it at easier difficulties are in opposition to the game encouraging you to try and push for harder challenged. "Hard mode" is then replaced with things like 窶徘lay on difficulty 30窶・or 窶徘lay with these modifiers窶・ 

That's not even going into fun things like how it can allow for daily runs or challenges based around specific modifiers.
