Hey big fan of the game but I have some feedback regarding unlockable skills 

Many of the 20-skull unlockable skills look really fun and cool, but I almost never get to play with them due to a few reasons:
1. Game encourages stacking upgrades on 1 or 2 skills, so players naturally upgrade skills they get early (skills found late are usually sacrificed/not used)
2. Players are only offered one skill per biome and there are more weak/boring skills than fun/cool ones, so there aren't many chances to see the cool skills 
3. Gold is better spent on buying/rerolling upgrades than on rerolling for skills
4. It feels like there's a rarity system for skills such that fun and cool skills appear less often, especially early on

Because of this, most of my runs are very similar, where I focus on upgrading my starting skill plus whatever skill I picked up early (usually a weak/boring skill)

It's such a shame since many of the later skills are really cool but I never properly use them

My suggestions to improve this situation:
1. Increase number of skill choices to 3, more player choice = more agency on our builds = more fun
2. Sell skills on shops (except for Sacrifice shops)
3. Offer players upgraded skills (upgrades can be random) for later stages, so they are not completely useless

Hopefully my feedback is helpful, happy to have a conversation about it ・
