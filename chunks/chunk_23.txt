Two enemy behavior ideas, perhaps applicable as a toggleable difficulty increase unlocked at some point which adds to skull earnings? There is a lot here though it's sort of unrealistic, 1st enemy behavior idea on its own would probably only be used by introductory bosses, and then mostly in conjunction with the 2nd one which is meant to drastically increase the amount of thinking needed during bossfights.

**Guard** - Instead of telegraphing an action, apply damage resistance that lasts until the end of player turn. Damage resistance can be a certain value/be invulnerability.

I think it would be most fitting and easiest to implement as behavior primarily used by bosses as balancing would only need to be accounted for in just that one encounter, enemies reappear onwards from their introduction so at most it would be utilized in rare scenarios (ex. shielder finished shielding everything including itself).

Potential outcomes from being hit during guard:
Nothing (upon health loss, indicated by blue flashing) - Damage resistance takes effect.
Stagger (upon health loss, indicated by yellow double-flashing) - If health loss occurs, perform notable behavior. Tiles ex. Tetsubo could be buffed to always pierce damage resistance and stagger
Counter (red outline and rapid vibration) - Perform notable behavior if no health loss is experienced
